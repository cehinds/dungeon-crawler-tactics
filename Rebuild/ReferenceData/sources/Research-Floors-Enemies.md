# Floor and enemy research

Research checked 23 October 2026. **Spoilers through Book 6** in the detailed floor notes; the reserved-floor table includes a few later published setting labels. This is a source brief, not an implementation claim. [Machine-readable research](../Research/floors-enemies.json) records sources, confidence, enemies and proposed adaptations separately.

Publisher descriptions and available samples are primary evidence for broad settings. Fan databases supply most detailed mechanics and chapter locators; those details are secondary summaries, not independent checks of the complete novels. Several Fandom pages block direct retrieval, so indexed page text was used. Nothing here reproduces novel dialogue.

## Corrections that affect campaign design

- **Floor 7 is the Great Race, but its normal traversal is bypassed.** Prepotente destroys the designed structure. A playable full-length race would be a counterfactual adaptation, not a recreation of a race the party completed. [Great Race](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/seventh_floor/)
- **Floor 8 is the card floor.** Its Earth-memory environment and T’Ghee capture/combat system are separate parts of the same setting. The publisher explicitly describes collecting six monsters as cards. It is not another ordinary dungeon-room floor. [Publisher description](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/788703/the-eye-of-the-bedlam-bride-by-matt-dinniman/hardcover/)
- **Floor 5 uses Land, Sea, Air and Subterranean quadrants.** Do not substitute an unsourced fire/ice/lightning resistance scheme. [Publisher sample](https://cdn.penguin.co.uk/dam-assets/books/9780241829912/9780241829912-sample.pdf)

## Floors 1–8

| Floor / book | Source-supported structure | Useful adaptation, explicitly invented |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Book 1** | Tutorial passage networks and mob neighborhoods; local boss defeat stops neighborhood spawning. [First Floor](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/First_Floor) | Small lessons in movement, terrain and boss warnings. Hoarder adds, Juicer cover and Speedbump construction can use visible bounded turn rules. |
| **2 · Book 1** | Second training floor. Brindle Grubs feed on corpses and develop into more dangerous forms. [Second Floor](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Floor), [grubs](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/brindle_grub/) | A bounded growth/pressure meter with a clear departure objective. Cleanup affects growth, not the existence of new spawns. |
| **3 · Book 2** | Over City: guarded settlements and dangerous ruins; race/class choice begins here, with quests feeding the linked storyline. [Over City](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Floor) | Branch between safe settlements and infected circus ruins; make quest consequences explicit. |
| **4 · Book 3** | Iron Tangle: tangled railway navigation, trains, platforms and transfers; stairs open only during the final six hours. [Publisher](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/772014/the-dungeon-anarchists-cookbook-by-matt-dinniman/hardcover/), [floor rules](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Floor) | Connected train-car maps, route selection and rescue missions. A turn countdown is an adaptation of the narrative clock. |
| **5 · Book 4** | Isolated bubbles require cooperative conquest of four castles. All four must fall before the castle stairwells become usable. [Publisher](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/788551/the-gate-of-the-feral-gods-by-matt-dinniman/), [stairs](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Stairwells) | Four linked objective maps with shared progress and environmental mobility constraints. |
| **6 · Book 5** | Hunting Grounds: jungle-covered urban ruins; outworld tourists hunt crawlers. [Publisher](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/788552/the-butchers-masquerade-by-matt-dinniman/paperback/), [floor setting](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Sixth_Floor) | Pursuit, ambush, rescue and extraction encounters with readable hunter loadouts. |
| **7 · Book 6** | Intended one-way tube maze, branching danger levels and pitstops; the structure is destroyed before ordinary traversal. [Great Race](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/seventh_floor/) | A short transition acknowledging the bypass. Leave a normal enemy roster unspecified. |
| **8 · Book 6** | Ghosts of Earth: Earth-memory regions with intangible inhabitants and tangible objects. Capture precedes an assault for exit keys. [Floor account](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/eighth_floor/) | Two distinct modes: collecting a viable squad, then an authored card encounter. Simplified deck rules must be labeled. |

The overall dungeon has 18 floors; 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 and 18 participate in the linked volcano storyline. This relationship does not establish a full enemy roster for every linked floor. [World Dungeon](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon)

## Enemy identity and verified counterplay

| Enemy / location | Supported counterplay or limitation | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Hoarder / Floor 1** | Human-ish garbage boss with Scatterers; not a treasure-chest mimic. [Hoarder](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/hoarder/) | Capped emergence/fire interruption are tactical inventions. |
| **Juicer / Floor 1** | Scaly muscular gym boss with flaming weight throws; the account identifies vulnerable high-pressure veins. [Juicer](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/juicer/) | A non-graphic cover/telegraph encounter preserves identity without reproducing the novel’s injuries. |
| **Ball of Swine / Floor 1** | A rolling body stopped by the cooperative Speedbump contraption. [Ball of Swine](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Ball_of_Swine) | Treat this as engineering counterplay, not an elemental weakness. |
| **Danger Dingo / Floor 2** | Large fanged canine, sometimes with a Kobold rider. No universal weakness established here. [Danger Dingo](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/danger_dingo/) | Pounce/pack/rider statistics would be invented. |
| **Brindle Grub / Floor 2** | Destroying corpses inhibits feeding and growth but **does not stop spawning**. [Brindle Grub](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/brindle_grub/) | Avoid a false “burn corpses to stop all grubs” instruction. |
| **Pestiferous Vine / Floors 3 and 6** | Fungus/parasite hybrid; poisoned blood is a delayed vulnerability, and curing that blood can permit recovery. [Pestiferous Vine](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/pestiferous_vine/) | Do not infer a fire weakness merely from the word “vine.” |
| **Village guards / Floor 3** | Protect population centers. Hostility is contextual. [Guard swordsman](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/village_guard_swordsman/) | Support faction-sensitive NPC behavior. |
| **Jikininki / Floor 4** | Janitor ghouls can be provoked by bleeding, litter and trespass. [Jikininki](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/jikininki_janitor_ghouls/) | An avoidance rule can matter more than a damage weakness. |
| **Station Mimic / Floor 4** | Severed pieces return; removing more than half its mass prevents healing/transformation. Bashing and magic are ineffective in the cited account. [Station Mimic](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/station_mimic/) | A severance/containment objective fits better than arbitrary elemental bonuses. |
| **Night Fright / Concierge Shark · Floor 5** | Listed in Air/Water regions; a detailed weakness was not established. [Floor roster](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Floor) | Flying/aquatic roles are design proposals until individual rules are sourced. |
| **Hunters / Floor 6** | Visiting outworlders hunt crawlers for gear. No shared race-wide weakness established. [Hunters](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Hunters) | Readable equipment archetypes instead of a generic weakness tag. |
| **Regional folklore / Floor 8** | Examples include Romanian vampires/werewolves and Irish leprechauns/banshees. [Floor account](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/eighth_floor/) | Folklore stereotypes do not automatically establish DCC silver/sunlight weaknesses. |

Big Tina’s Floor 6 dinosaur-attack quest is an additional encounter anchor, not evidence of a universal dinosaur stat block. [Big Tina](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/big_tina/)
