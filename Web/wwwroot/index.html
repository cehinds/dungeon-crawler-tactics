Welcome, crawler.

Three encounters. Four allies. Every attack forecast before you commit.

Survive the first floor.

  1. Prepare at camp. Choose your deployed crawlers and classes, then enter the dungeon. Mordecai stays behind as your camp advisor.
  2. Explore the passages. Click a highlighted path or use arrow keys / WASD. Approach discoveries to investigate supplies, read notes, or enter an encounter.
  3. Select a crawler. The bottom strip shows turn order. Read the selected crawler’s health, mana, movement and class actions in the HUD.
  4. Move, then use an ability. Click Move and choose a highlighted tile, or choose an ability and its highlighted target. Range, mana and valid teams come from the shared rules.
  5. Read the forecast. A confirmation dialog shows the target’s exact HP, mana and status changes. Choose Confirm to act, or Cancel to return without spending resources. Confirm spends the action and ends that character’s turn; cancel spends nothing.
  6. End this character’s turn. Choose Actions to compare abilities, costs and legal targets. End character turn opens a confirmation showing who acts next; confirm it to finish without an ability. Confirming an ability also ends that character’s turn. The next crawler can act, then the enemies take their turn. Clear the room, collect a sponsor reward and return to camp.
