Welcome, crawler.

Three encounters. Four allies. Every attack forecast before you commit.

Survive the first floor.

  1. Build a party of four. Choose crawlers and classes in camp, then explore the dungeon.
  2. Explore the floor. Click blue outlined paths or use arrow keys / WASD to reveal the map. Approach a cache, field note, or encounter, then use its interaction button. Return to camp to adjust your party.
  3. Move, then act. Select a crawler. Blue outlined tiles show where they can move. Abilities show their effects and only legal targets are highlighted. Each crawler has one move and one action per round.
  4. Choose an action and its target. Click an enemy, ally, or tile. The forecast shows the exact result. Confirm spends the action; cancel spends nothing.
  5. End your turn. Enemies move and attack, hazards tick, and the next round begins. Defeat every enemy to win.
  6. Claim a sponsor reward. Recover in camp and advance through three encounters. A fallen party can retry the current encounter.

Look for reactions

FIRE + OILIgnition Burns away oil and leaves fire.

LIGHTNING + WETConduction Extra damage on a wet target.

SHATTER + FROZENShatter Breaks ice for bonus damage.

Preview before committing: immunities, surfaces, energy, and reactions are included. Some spells target terrain directly; use tile targeting to change a surface beneath an enemy.
