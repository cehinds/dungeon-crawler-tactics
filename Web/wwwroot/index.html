Explore, then fight.

Exploration: Hold WASD, arrow keys, or the direction pad to walk. Press E or Space beside a discovery to interact. Generated dungeons start combat when enemies are nearby; after winning and choosing your reward, continue exploring and reach the exit. All gameplay maps use square tiles.

  1. Prepare at camp. Visit Mordecai’s safe room. Rest to restore health and mana, equip owned gear for actual stat bonuses, and inspect each crawler’s Skills and level unlocks. Choose your deployed crawlers and enter the dungeon.
  2. Explore the passages. Hold the direction pad, arrow keys or WASD to walk. Approach discoveries to investigate supplies, read notes, or enter an encounter. The Quest button tracks progress. Rotate the map with the 90° controls; arrow keys and WASD follow the current camera view.
  3. Select a crawler. The bottom strip shows turn order. Tap the selected portrait to open any crawler’s character sheet, stats, stances, forms, skills, level progress and equipment. Read the selected crawler’s health, mana, movement and class actions in the HUD.
  4. Move and act in either order. Click Move and choose a highlighted tile, or open Abilities & skills, choose an ability and select its highlighted target. The selected ability details stay visible while targeting. Range, mana and valid teams come from the shared rules.
  5. Read the forecast. A confirmation dialog shows the target’s exact HP, mana and status changes. Choose Confirm to act, or Cancel to return without spending resources. Confirm spends one action; movement is separate and remains available if unused. Cancel spends nothing.
  6. End this character’s turn. Choose Actions to compare abilities, costs and legal targets. End character turn opens a confirmation showing who acts next; confirm it to finish without an ability. An ability does not end the character’s turn. Move or act in either order, then explicitly end it. The next crawler can act, then the enemies visibly move and attack. Actions stay locked during the enemy phase; Skip animation is optional. Clear the room, collect a sponsor reward and return to camp.
