# Map structure pass

This checklist tracks reported map-composition defects in the concept-art room generator. It covers the illustrative map and its generated art only; it does not define gameplay, movement rules, or production level design. Findings below are open until the implementation and QA evidence are recorded. Do not reopen the accepted general floor, wall, water, portal-socket, or room-generation criteria from Pass 2 unless this pass demonstrates a specific regression.

## Composition model

The room is assembled from a tile layer and object layers. Tile cells describe the floor, water, and structural boundary; edge tags constrain which neighboring tile edges may meet. Boundary caps, wall faces, corners, piers, and portal frames are separate structure placements with explicit bounds and anchors. Props and actors occupy authored cell footprints, while upright art may extend beyond those cells; its base anchor controls overlap order. The logical opening and landing are recorded independently from the portal image rectangle so a large illustration cannot silently redefine the room boundary.

For each placement, the recipe should carry enough data to validate its cell footprint, render bounds, anchor, layer/depth behavior, and whether it blocks connectivity. Room validation should check the composed result: in-bounds geometry, compatible tile/structure joins, portal-to-opening alignment, unobstructed required paths, actor/prop placement, and the HUD boundary. Keep fixes limited to these map-structure contracts and visible composition; combat behavior and general gameplay mechanics are outside this pass.

## Scope guard

Pass 2 already records accepted checks for reusable floor modules, wall faces/caps/corners/piers, water banks and outlets, portal alignment to logical traversal cells, deterministic room composition, and the board/HUD boundary. This pass may adjust their use to resolve the defects above, but must not replace those accepted results with generic unfinished claims. Any newly authored or extended art must retain its derived/source provenance and must not claim recovery of pixels hidden in the flattened reference.

## Verification status

Independent map-structure QA passed 1,007 seeds with a repeated deterministic stream, published-plan parity, 17 geometry/socket fixtures, eight fixed-HUD mismatch fixtures, and four malformed-crop fixtures. The canonical reconstruction passed with zero source-pixel differences; all 1,319 frozen component, manifest, and derived-catalog hashes remained unchanged. Canonical room 9 and previews 7, 29, and 103 received bounded visual review. Browser QA then passed seeds 7, 9, 29, and 103 at desktop (1440 × 1000) and mobile (390 × 844): canvas pixels matched the Python renders, downloaded PNG/JSON matched the display and generated room plans, and there were no page errors or horizontal overflow.
