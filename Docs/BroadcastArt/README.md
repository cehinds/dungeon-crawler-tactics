# Broadcast chamber and safe-house modular art

This is a two-pass art study combining the navy/gold GBA Command Deck direction with cyan/magenta Dungeon Broadcast feedback. Both concepts use an orthogonal square grid. Combat and exploration have separate concept scenes and separate modular production sheets; concept compositions are not cropped into tiles.

The inventory contains 48 generated source components (24 per pass) plus 13 derived structural variants, for 61 exported pieces. Runtime-size exports are 128×128 PNGs. Terrain fills its square canvas. Props and feedback overlays retain alpha with a bottom-center anchor `(64,128)` and up to 112×112 visible content, leaving side padding. Structural wall variants instead reach their declared cell edges with canonical 64px bands. Source wall/road junction art remains available as explicitly unverified studies. Source generation files are preserved unmodified in AI-Art `dungeon-crawler-tactics/originals/broadcast-kit/`; working copies live in ignored `Web/wwwroot/assets/broadcast-kit/`.

Rebuild with `python3 Tools/build_broadcast_art.py` after restoring source sheets. `crops.json` is the authored crop source of truth. The exporter records exact source rectangles, SHA-256, content bounds and alpha extrema in `manifest.json`, then assembles `qa-sheet.png`. Asset IDs are stable. Each entry describes layer, anchor, tile dimensions, adjacency category and four cardinal edge tags. `manifest.schema.json` describes the browser-gallery contract.

The sole authored rotation is 0°. No rotation is promised for doors, stairs, wall faces or props. Cardinal edge labels describe intended connections only; they do not prove seamless joins, collision, traversal, or WFC support. The 13 derived canonical wall connectors pass exact 64px alpha-edge interval tests; see [QA.md](QA.md). Terrain color edges and source junction shapes require assembled-map review before production use. Wet-looking art and oil art are decorative until mapped to canonical game terrain. No mechanics or gameplay integration is provided by this asset batch.
