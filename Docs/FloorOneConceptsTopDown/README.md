# Floor One top-down concept revision

This is a separate revision of the same 36 Floor 1 concept panels documented in [coverage.json](coverage.json). It preserves the approved mixed-camera package under [FloorOneConcepts](../FloorOneConcepts/README.md); it does not replace or modify that set. The coverage IDs, order, evidence statuses, source links, and six batches of six are carried over unchanged.

## Projection review

Root has visually approved the six sheets as direct-overhead top-down scene layouts across all 36 panels. Thin interior wall sides and prop depth can remain, and some details are stylized for readability; this is not a mathematically verified nadir projection. Keep that qualification when describing the images. The review and claim scope are recorded in `scope.projectionReview` in the coverage file.

The images remain environment concepts, not tile-ready assets or playable maps. Room dimensions, object placement, connections, and other details absent from sources are inferred. The canon and visit-status caveats in coverage still apply, including the relationship between the false guild alley and Dozer encounter, the sub-locations within larger venues, and the mentioned-only or generic panels.

## Art and source boundaries

Use the same 36 IDs and status semantics as the original set. Keep the environments focused; named party-character designs and Book 2 locations or characters are outside scope. Creature details remain illustrative unless independently verified. Do not rename generic territories as canon towns or present an inferred layout as an authoritative floorplan.

The previous mixed-camera images and package remain intact. This revision is a parallel visual interpretation so reviewers can compare camera treatment without losing the approved originals.

## Coverage

The records derive from the [Book 1–2 location research](../BookLocationTiles/research-books-1-2.json) and the previous [36-panel coverage](../FloorOneConcepts/coverage.json). The selection is evidence-backed, not an exhaustive concordance or a complete survey of every location in the novel.

Verify extracted files against `package-manifest.json` if needed.
