# Katia and Mongo appearance research

Research date: 2026-10-31. Art-only research; no asset changes or publication. Evidence distinguishes book descriptions reported by a secondary source from independently inspected primary text.

## Katia: select a named presentation

[Fandom appearance section and references](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Katia_Grim#Appearance) report these book locators:

| Presentation | Supported brief | Book locator reported by source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original human | Adult woman, mid-thirties; short black hair; pale skin; light, wide-set eyes. Exact iris color and original height/build unverified. | *Carl's Doomsday Scenario*, epilogue |
| First meeting | An imperfectly sculpted humanoid doppelgänger; blonde presentation, blue tracksuit and snow boots. | Book 2, chapter 21 |
| Purple punk combat presentation | Purple mohawk, leather outfit, black boots. A large muscular transformation is separately described. | *The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*, chapter 15; eight-foot form: chapter 31 |

Short source quote: “short black hair, pale skin”. This is a secondary transcription, not a directly inspected book passage. The wiki's hair infobox separately assigns purple to Book 3 and blond to Books 4–7; do not assume one permanent appearance across the series. Human-shaped rounded ears are the conservative art choice: no inspected source establishes pointed elf ears. Exact ear anatomy, exact eye hue, body measurements, leather cut, and later hairstyle details remain unverified. Avoid depicting an elf race merely because she can shapeshift.

Actionable decision: for a natural/base portrait use short black hair and pale human features; for the recognizable Book 3 battle sprite use the purple **mohawk**, leather and black boots, with an explicitly labeled form. Purple hair itself is not an error; the fantasy-elf silhouette and treating that presentation as her original body are the problems.

User-directed battle target: the same pale human Katia, very tall and strongly muscled, purple mohawk, **no green skin**. “She-Hulk” describes the muscular scale, not permission to copy Marvel's green character. [SuperSummary's chapters 6–10 guide](https://www.supersummary.com/the-dungeon-anarchists-cookbook/chapters-6-10-summary/) places the purple mohawk/black bodysuit styling in chapter 6, earlier than Fandom's chapter-15 locator. The reliable shared conclusion is Book 3; exact first occurrence needs primary-text confirmation.

## Mongo: dinosaur anatomy, then age/form

[Fandom appearance section and references](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Mongo#Appearance) reports:

| Stage | Supported art brief | Book locator reported by source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earliest hatchling | Seven-inch Mongoliensis/velociraptor; pink down and red accents; small forearms, long tail, beaked mouth containing teeth. | *Dungeon Crawler Carl*, chapter 46; Book 2, chapter 5 |
| Grown mount | Pony-height, approximately thirteen feet including a tail about half the length; blue/red tail feathers; powerful clawed hind feet. Black collar; optional blue/red tasselled saddle. | *The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*, chapter 7; collar: Book 2, chapter 5 |

Short source quote: “two tiny forearms instead of wings”. The wiki reports later wings, including a Book 6 chapter 72 reference, but does **not** establish when forearms first became described as wings. Do not promote that later chapter into an origin date.

Art direction: forward-leaning bipedal feathered raptor, long counterbalancing tail, toothed beak/snout, clawed dinosaur feet, short feathered forelimbs. Pink is foundational; red and blue are accents. Avoid a red parrot's upright perching body, rounded bird head, toothless hooked bill, or separate large back wings. Specific adult skin color, precise color distribution and wing span remain unverified here.

## Wing timing: unresolved, not a pterosaur transformation

[Reader discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/DungeonCrawlerCarl/comments/1iy09tb/question_about_the_new_book/) calls the wings vestigial forelimbs; that is reader interpretation, not primary evidence. It supports investigating arm/wing terminology, not inventing a wing-acquisition event in Books 3–4. No inspected evidence supports Mongo turning into a pterodactyl or a flying pterosaur.

For a later winged variant, keep the same raptor body and use feathered forelimb wings, not membranous bat/pterosaur wings. The exact onset and dimensions need a direct Book 3/4 text check before being labeled canon. Book 8's authorized excerpt, originally published by IGN, contains the short line “He waved his wings”; [the original article URL](https://www.ign.com/articles/dungeon-crawler-carl-matt-dinniman-interview-parade-of-horribles-excerpt) could not be fetched, while a [syndicated forum excerpt](https://vapingunderground.com/threads/dungeon-crawler-carl-matt-dinniman-interview-and-exclusive-a-parade-of-horribles-excerpt.4152567/) was indexed. Treat that as indirect corroboration only.

## Primary/official sources checked and limits

[PRH's Book 2 excerpt](https://www.penguinrandomhouseretail.com/book/?isbn=9780593820278) is directly accessible but its opening does not resolve these appearance questions. Its black bat-wing description belongs to **Mordecai**, not Mongo.

[Renegade's official Royal Court miniature set](https://renegadegamestudios.com/dungeon-crawler-carl-rpg-royal-court-minis-set/) explicitly lists Baby Mongo, Mongo Royal Court, Donut Riding Mongo and Katia. This establishes an official adaptation reference, not that every sculpt detail is stated in the novels. No uninspected miniature detail or cover detail is used as book proof here. Other search results included Bookworm AI, fan art, fan fiction and merchandise; none is elevated to primary canon.
