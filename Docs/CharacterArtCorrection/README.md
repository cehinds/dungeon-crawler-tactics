# Character art correction

This directory contains a prepared correction brief and reference links. **No character art has been generated.** The planned deliverables are single poses; walking animation is outside this brief. There is no completed-art count or pack manifest yet.

## Evidence guide

Use [`sources.json`](sources.json) to distinguish evidence types:

- **Primary text**: a book passage or publisher sample supports only details directly present in that text.
- **Licensed illustration**: official tabletop material is an adaptation reference, not proof of novel wording. The linked set image has not been visually reviewed for this brief; do not infer character features from it here.
- **Secondary index**: a wiki or database reports descriptions and book locators. Treat those details as reported until checked against the books.
- **User-directed design choice**: requested art direction, recorded separately from book evidence.

## Correction brief

- **Carl:** the user-directed correction is clean-shaven. The DCC Database gives secondary-source context, reporting that he shaves to avoid resembling his abusive father. The same index reports short brown hair, a very large build of about 6 ft 3 in, and an early outfit of jacket, shirt, cloak, boxers and bare feet.
- **Donut:** the Book 1 publisher sample, Chapter 1 printed pages 1–4, directly supports a flat tortoiseshell Persian cat with black, beige and white coloring. Yellow eyes are reported by a secondary source.
- **Katia:** the requested design is a purple mohawk and the same human-pale skin in her strongly muscled form; do not make her green. The eight-foot height is reported by a secondary source, not independently verified here. Keep this as a distinct presentation rather than assuming it is her original form.
- **Mongo:** consult book illustrations and official tabletop art as references, and do not give him a parrot design. The linked official image has not been visually reviewed here, so this brief makes no claim about its depicted anatomy. The research notes report a raptor-like book presentation; wing timing remains unverified, and no pterodactyl transformation should be claimed.

## Review checklist

1. Check the character and presentation against the correction brief and available source notes.
2. Keep user-directed design choices separate from book claims and secondary reports.
3. Treat official images as reference links only; do not include publisher samples, official illustrations or product images in any pack.
4. When art is produced, verify the deliverable is a single pose and make no animation claim.
