# Personal housing, training and club research

Checked 23 October 2026. [Structured findings and source URLs](../Research/housing.json). `null` prices mean **unknown**, never free. This brief supplies lore constraints; it does not claim the game already implements them.

The user’s housing distinction is supported: personal rooms can join a communal home while retaining individual ownership. A safe-room service menu is not the whole housing system. Owners control visitor access; only same-party spaces combine. Environmental benefits follow their purchaser, while table upgrades stay with their table. [Personal Space](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/personal_space/)

## Prices with evidence

The **official Book 3 sample, printed pages 36–40**, confirms the following offers. [Publisher sample](https://cdn.penguin.co.uk/dam-assets/books/9780241816240/9780241816240-sample.pdf)

| Purchase | Gold |
|---|---:|
| Joining fee, each union | 5,000 |
| Tier-one Training Room | 250,000 |
| Enhanced Crafting Studio | **255,000** |
| Ultra-Stabilized adjustable bed | 250,000 |
| Social Screen | 50,000 |
| Quest Screen | 90,000 |
| Adventurers Inc. jobs access | 100,000 |
| Invite-only Naughty Boys agency | 500,000 |

The sample also confirms purchaser identity, shared benefits, room withdrawal, and the 250,000-gold coupon limit without coupon stacking. Acquisition occurs on **Floor 4 / Book 3**, not Floor 3. The broader purchase system is available from Floor 3. [Personal Space](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/personal_space/)

Additional secondary offers: base housing 50,000, Donut’s negotiated example 37,500; Cleaner Bot 25,000; Engineer’s Table **approximately** 25,000. These do not establish a level-three housing price. [Wendita](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/wendita/), [Cleaner Bot](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Cleaner_Bot), [Engineer’s Table](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/engineer%27s_table/)

Shower, Bomber’s Studio, Sapper’s Table, Alchemy Table, Vocal Coach and club-membership prices remain unverified in this review.

## Training, tools and social access

- **Training Room:** tier one permits one hour daily on an eligible nonmagical skill; explosive practice requires the later Bomber’s Studio. Do not conflate a personal practice room with every specialized guild. [Training Room](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Training_Room)
- **Guild example:** Dodge 7 grants eligibility. Raising it to 8 is initially free; subsequent quoted raises cost 20,000 for 9 and 250,000 for 10. These are specific offers, not a universal skill-price formula. [Dodge Skill](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/dodge_skill/)
- **Crafting:** Carl’s Sapper’s Table is earned in Book 1 / Floor 2 and installed in the later home. Tables provide specialized functions and skill gates; they are not automatic fixtures of all safe rooms. [Sapper’s Table](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/sapper%27s_table/)
- **Equipment certification:** an Engineering Table can fabricate armor-shaped objects, but the described process also needs an Armorer’s Table before they count as equipped armor. [Engineer’s Table](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/engineer%27s_table/)
- **Social Screen:** a purchased AI-moderated audience feed, not unrestricted external internet access. Its later use is documented in Book 4 / Floor 5. [Social Media Board](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Social_Media_Board)
- **Upgrade timing:** tier two begins on Floor 6. Not every useful room belongs in a starting-floor home. [Personal Space](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/personal_space/)

## Clubs: credentials, not a universal purchase

Desperado’s public front pub and members-only interior have different access rules. The inner club uses earned personal membership, is not itself a safe room, excludes pets, and returns visitors to their own entrance rather than enabling fast travel. Internal tiers open with Floors 3, 6 and 9. [Desperado Club](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Desperado_Club)

Standard Desperado access excludes a Vanquisher pass and can remove Cleric/Paladin class options. Exceptions exist: certain gods or bard classes allow access across that divide. Do not implement the ordinary restriction as an exceptionless rule. [Class restrictions](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/list_of_classes/), [Club Vanquisher](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Club_Vanquisher)

Carl’s Emberus affiliation gives **limited** Vanquisher access, with paperwork and one area per day. This benefit does not establish permanent all-party access. The exact general transferability of every club credential was not established; the safe implementation boundary is per-crawler eligibility, not a shared inventory unlock. [Emberus temple](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Emberus_High_Temple_of_the_Ending_Sun)

No separate **Anarchist Club** was established by this review. The Compensated Anarchist class includes Desperado access; the secret Cookbook is a different system whose exposure can cost its holder access. [Compensated Anarchist](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Compensated_Anarchist_Class), [Cookbook item](https://www.dccdatabase.com/wiki/dungeon_anarchist%27s_cookbook_%28item%29/)
