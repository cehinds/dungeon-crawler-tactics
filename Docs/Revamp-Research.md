# Revamp research and content boundaries

This content pack adapts the named characters into distinct tactical identities. Character labels and source facts are grounded in the books; ability effects, costs, range, and turn behavior are game conversions. The interface uses the identity class names without appending a disclaimer to every action. This document records which parts are source anchors and which are invented mechanics.

## Source notes

The primary source checked is the publisher's official sample for *Carl's Doomsday Scenario* (Dungeon Crawler Carl, Book 2), ISBN 9780241816219: [Penguin Random House UK sample PDF](https://cdn.penguin.co.uk/dam-assets/books/9780241816219/9780241816219-sample.pdf). Relevant passages identify Carl's Compensated Anarchist class and explosive/trap improvisation, Princess Donut's Former Child Actor class and learned Magic Missile, and Mordecai as a noncombatant (p. 12); Donut's Magic Missile is described as a permanent learned ability on pp. 19–20. The sample also describes Mongo as a baby velociraptor on PDF page 14 (zero-based page 13). These are narrow early-series anchors, not a full-series lore source.

Secondary references include [Princess Donut](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Donut), [Katia's Doppelgänger page](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Doppelg%C3%A4nger), and [Mongo](https://www.novelpantheon.com/novels/dungeon-crawler-carl/characters/mongo). Reserve-crawler descriptions below rely on secondary summaries: [Elle McGib](https://www.novelpantheon.com/novels/dungeon-crawler-carl/characters/elle-mcgib), [Imani](https://www.novelpantheon.com/novels/dungeon-crawler-carl/characters/imani), [Louis](https://www.novelpantheon.com/novels/dungeon-crawler-carl/characters/louis), and [Britney](https://www.novelpantheon.com/novels/dungeon-crawler-carl/characters/britney). The [official Book 4 sample](https://cdn.penguin.co.uk/dam-assets/books/9780241829912/9780241829912-sample.pdf) includes Elle and Imani but does not establish the full appearance details below. Secondary pages may include later-book spoilers; this game's art direction uses only the bounded traits noted here. They help identify terms such as Puddle Jumper, Heal Critter, and Mongo's species name, *Velociraptor mongoliensis*. Novel Pantheon's character summary says Mongo's forelimbs develop feathered wings and describes red/pink plumage with blue eye highlights (chapters 46–81). The exact first-appearance book within that range has not been independently verified; the official Book 4 sample ([PDF](https://cdn.penguin.co.uk/dam-assets/books/9780241829912/9780241829912-sample.pdf)) does not readily confirm the wing details. Treat these as secondary references; they do not establish the tactical effects shipped here.

## Character identity and game mechanics

- **Carl — Compensated Anarchist:** The class identity and his explosive/trap improvisation are source-grounded. `Powerful Strike` and `Improvised Bomb` are descriptive game actions. The current bomb lights one tile and can trigger the existing oil reaction; it does not do area damage.
- **Princess Donut — Former Child Actor:** The class identity and learned `Magic Missile` come from the official sample. This game's Magic Missile is Arcane damage and does not set up Wet/Lightning reactions. `Puddle Jumper` and `Heal Critter` are source-inspired labels; their tile teleport and ally-healing behavior are game conversions.
- **Katia — Doppelganger:** Doppelganger is her source-grounded race/identity, used here as the class name. Her `Shapeshift`, `Heavy Blow`, and `New Look` effects and stats are invented tactical conversions, not claims about exact book mechanics.
- **Mongo — Raptor Companion:** The Book 2 sample identifies Mongo as a baby velociraptor. A secondary chapter summary describes later feathered wings and red/pink plumage; the authored art uses that appearance direction without asserting flight or a pterodactyl identity. `Mongo Bite` and `Mongo Pounce` are descriptive game actions; the damage and teleport rules are invented mechanics.
- **Mordecai:** The publisher sample identifies him as a noncombatant (p. 12). He remains a nondeployable advisor. The default field party is Carl, Donut, Katia, and Mongo.
- **Elle:** Secondary summaries describe her as a 99-year-old Meadow Lark resident who becomes a small, light-blue-skinned, white-haired hovering fairy; her Blizzardmancer identity is also from those summaries. Sprite direction uses the transformation's recognizable silhouette without reproducing late-book plot details. Ice Shard and Glacial Lock are single-target cold-control game skills; their numbers and frozen effect are invented.
- **Imani:** Secondary summaries identify her as a night-shift caregiver, later a Fire Spiritualist and Obsidian Butterfly. Scrubs and a longsword are early visual cues; the art uses her later butterfly-wing silhouette as a mixed-series visual choice, without depicting its plot reveal. Wing Guard, Steady Hands, and Triage are invented single-ally guard/heal actions, not exact named book abilities.
- **Louis:** Secondary summaries connect him to Miami, a van, and a Pest Exterminator class, with gas equipment and goggles in later class descriptions. The gameplay's Exhaust Cloud and Fumigate are single-target damage/status conversions using existing effects; they do not create an area cloud or summon.
- **Britney (stable save ID `crawler.brittany`):** The secondary page spells her name Britney and describes dark hair, a fur outfit, and a spike-covered club; it associates her with Pit Fighter. The data retains the legacy stable ID while using the source spelling in the display name. Spike Club and the later guard/swipe skills are invented mechanics. Late-series scars and plot details are excluded.

## Mechanical scope

Identity class grants and personal innate grants point to the same stable ability IDs; the resolver deduplicates them. Push moves a target away from the actor until blocked; Teleport moves the actor to a legal tile in range. Arcane has no registered reactions. Water followed by Lightning still demonstrates Wet/Conductivity through the existing Coolant Bomb and Shock Tonic items, and Cold/Frozen/Shatter remains available through the existing items and abilities. Content and saves retain the internal `energy` fields and stable IDs for compatibility.

The floor has three authored encounters plus optional caches and notes. Progression thresholds are cumulative XP `[0, 30, 70, 120]`; party-wide level bonuses add 3 HP and 1 MP per level, and character-specific skills unlock automatically at levels 2 and 3. Six equipment records provide small HP, MP, or movement bonuses and become available by encounter count. Gear is equipped in the safe-room/camp flow. The game's safe room allows resting to restore each crawler's HP and MP, equipping owned gear, and reviewing skills before the next encounter. Equipment availability is derived from the number of completed encounters. The quest journal derives progress from the authored floor-exit goal, discovered sector door, claimed caches/notes, and current encounter enemy defeats; it does not invent parallel counters or plot events.

## Resource terminology

Player-facing resource terminology is **Mana**, abbreviated **MP** in compact HUD labels. Ability costs are mana costs. The content schema and save format retain the established `energy` field names for compatibility. There is no authored Energy/EN label; legacy internal field names remain unchanged for saves and content compatibility.
